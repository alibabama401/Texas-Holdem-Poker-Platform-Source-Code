Private rooms and tables

SNG and MTT tournaments

dz.proto / GameRecord.protoTexas Holdem message fields and game-record structures.

Public scope: the repository contains selected C++ callbacks, headers, Protobuf definitions, Unity metadata, and product media. It does not include a complete dependency graph, build scripts, database, service entry points, Unity scenes, or an administration frontend. Treat it as a source and protocol reference rather than a deployable full platform.

05

Frequently asked questions

Can it be compiled and deployed immediately?

No. The public files do not form a complete project and omit dependencies, databases, configuration, and client scenes.

Does it include a payment system?

Protocols include coin and entry-fee fields, but no payment-provider integration is verifiable, so payment-system is not used as a Topic.

Why remove competitor brand terms?

Other poker brands are not this repository’s name. Brand terms weaken topical accuracy and may mislead searchers.
